ProVerbs Privacy Policy

Draft for the first public monetised release · Last revised: 16 September 2026

Status: This page is a draft prepared for the first public ProVerbs release that includes Google AdMob interstitial advertising and an optional Apple ad-free subscription. As of this revision, the iOS project has not yet linked the Google Mobile Ads SDK or Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) in a Release binary, and Release monetisation flags remain off. Do not treat this draft as the final published policy until the monetised build’s code, AdMob configuration, App Store Connect App Privacy answers, and this text have been checked against each other and this draft notice is removed.

This Privacy Policy describes how Diana Zaluzhna, operating as Akerez Software (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the ProVerbs iOS application and this website (akerez.com). Akerez Software is a brand name used by Diana Zaluzhna; it is not confirmed here as a separately registered company.

Contact: support@akerez.com

1. Overview

ProVerbs is an educational app for practising French verb conjugations. It does not require an Akerez Software account and does not ask you to create a password. Practice history, custom exercises, settings and statistics are stored on your device.

In the monetised public release this draft describes, ProVerbs is intended to:

  • show an interstitial advertisement from Google AdMob before a user without an active ad-free entitlement starts a new exercise or assessment; and
  • offer an optional auto-renewable Apple subscription that removes advertising while keeping all learning features available without payment.

Advertising is not intended to appear before every question, during answer entry, or solely because you finish a session. If an ad fails to load or is dismissed, practice is intended to continue. Resume of an unfinished attempt is not intended to require a new interstitial.

This website is a static informational site. These privacy pages do not use cookies, analytics SDKs or advertising tags.

2. Information stored on your device

ProVerbs stores the following kinds of information locally on your iPhone using Apple frameworks such as SwiftData and UserDefaults:

  • custom exercises you create and visibility preferences for exercises
  • practice attempts, scores, mastery-related progress and study-time records
  • app settings such as interface language, appearance and practice aids (for example Hint Mode, Learning Mode and translation display)
  • optional learning-target preferences used by the statistics experience

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example by emailing support). Uninstalling ProVerbs, or deleting the app’s data through iOS, removes the local store.

We do not operate an Akerez Software user account system or sign-in, do not enable iCloud / CloudKit sync for ProVerbs practice data, and do not run a ProVerbs backend that receives your conjugations or statistics. Core learning features do not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Confirmed product intent for the monetised release: interstitial ads before starting a new exercise or assessment for users who are not entitled to the ad-free subscription; no intentional ads mid-question.

Implementation status (unconfirmed until the Release binary is verified): the shipping ProVerbs Xcode project does not yet include the Google Mobile Ads package dependency, production AdMob application / unit identifiers, or a completed Release enablement of advertising. The statements in this section about live SDK behaviour apply only after those items ship and this draft is finalised.

When advertising is enabled, ad technology is provided by Google LLC through Google AdMob. Depending on your region, consent choices, Apple’s platform rules and the live AdMob configuration, ads may involve personalised or non-personalised delivery. This policy does not claim that ads are always non-personalised, that no identifiers are used, or that “no data is collected.”

3.1 Information Google’s ads SDK may process

According to Google’s documentation for App Store data disclosure regarding the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK may collect certain information from apps, including (as described by Google):

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device
  • non-user-related crash logs and related diagnostic information
  • performance data such as app launch time, hang rate or energy usage
  • a device identifier, such as an advertising identifier or other app- or developer-bounded identifiers
  • advertising data, such as advertisements you have seen
  • product interaction information (for example taps or video views related to ads)

Google may use such information to serve and measure ads, improve advertising and related products, and for other purposes described in Google’s own policies. Optional AdMob features or mediation partners, if enabled in the shipping configuration, can involve additional data; this policy must be updated if that happens.

See Google’s AdMob iOS data disclosure guidance, the Google Privacy Policy, and Google’s advertising technologies information.

Diana Zaluzhna, operating as Akerez Software, does not independently operate Google’s ad servers and does not invent retention periods for data held by Google.

3.2 Purpose

Advertising is intended to support free access to ProVerbs. An active ad-free subscription (Section 5) is intended to remove advertising for entitled users.

4. Consent and privacy choices (EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland)

Confirmed AdMob console fact: a European regulations consent message has been created for ProVerbs and uses this Privacy Policy URL (https://akerez.com/privacy/).

Implementation status (unconfirmed until the Release binary is verified): Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK is not yet integrated in the ProVerbs app binary. In-app presentation of consent forms, checks such as whether ads may be requested, and a publisher privacy-options entry point are therefore not confirmed as present in a shipping build.

For the monetised release, the intended design is:

  • use Google UMP so that, where Google’s tooling requires it (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland as determined by Google), privacy choices are gathered before ads are requested;
  • request ads only when Google’s consent APIs indicate that requesting ads is allowed; and
  • when Google indicates that a privacy options entry point is required, provide a visible in-app control (planned location: Settings) that re-opens Google’s privacy options form.

You may also use Apple’s platform privacy controls where applicable, and any controls described by Google.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT): this draft does not state that ProVerbs uses ATT, and does not state that it never will. Whether an ATT prompt appears depends on the final shipping configuration and Apple’s rules. If ATT is enabled, this policy will be updated to describe it, including the purpose string shown to users. Until then, ATT use is unconfirmed.

5. Purchases and Apple subscriptions

The monetised release is intended to offer an optional auto-renewable subscription that removes advertising. Learning features remain available without a subscription.

Confirmed in the current ProVerbs codebase (StoreKit plumbing): the app includes StoreKit 2 logic for loading the product com.proverbs.ProVerbs.adfree.monthly, purchasing, restoring purchases, listening for transaction updates, refreshing entitlements, and opening Apple’s manage-subscriptions sheet. Release monetisation is still gated off until deliberately enabled for a public build.

When the subscription is offered publicly:

  • payment is processed by Apple, not by Akerez Software
  • Apple provides purchase and entitlement status to the app so advertising can be removed for entitled users
  • we do not receive your full payment card details
  • you can use Restore Purchases in the app when the subscription UI is enabled
  • you can manage or cancel in your Apple ID subscription settings

Apple’s handling of payment and Apple ID data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy. Manage subscriptions at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

More product help is available on the ProVerbs Support page.

6. Information you send when contacting support

If you email support@akerez.com or use the in-app feedback composer, you choose what to send. That may include your email address, device information, the ProVerbs version, a description of a problem, and optional screenshots or question details.

We use that information only to respond to your request and improve ProVerbs. Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers or other sensitive credentials.

7. Network activity and other third parties

Learning content used by ProVerbs (verb forms, meanings and sentence translations) is packaged with the app for offline use. An optional remote learning-pack download URL can be configured; it is not required for core offline practice.

When advertising and UMP are enabled in a released build, the app will communicate with Google’s advertising and consent services as described in Sections 3–4. System features you choose to use may also involve Apple services (for example Mail, App Store / StoreKit, backup, keyboard, Focus modes).

8. This website

The Akerez Software website is static HTML. These privacy pages do not set cookies, do not embed third-party analytics or advertising scripts, and do not include a contact form that posts data to an Akerez server. Your web host or browser may still produce ordinary technical logs (such as IP address and requested URL) according to that host’s practices.

This Privacy Policy URL is used as the policy link in Google AdMob privacy messaging. The page does not host ad tags or Funding Choices consent-message tags.

9. App Store privacy disclosures (alignment note)

After the Google Mobile Ads SDK (and UMP, if used) are integrated in the binary submitted to Apple, App Store Connect → App Privacy answers must be reviewed against:

  • Google’s data disclosure guidance (categories such as Device ID, Advertising Data, Product Interaction, Performance Data, Diagnostics, and coarse location derived from IP, as described by Google);
  • whether data is used for Tracking under Apple’s definition (including any ATT configuration);
  • Apple StoreKit purchase-related disclosures; and
  • the privacy manifest(s) embedded in the submitted archive.

Those App Store Connect answers are not filled in by this draft and must not be guessed in advance of the final SDK configuration.

10. Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to handle your request and maintain a minimal record of correspondence, then deleted or archived according to ordinary business practice.

We do not state retention periods for information processed by Google or Apple under their own services; those periods are governed by their respective policies.

11. Children

ProVerbs is an educational tool. Practice data created in the app remains on the device. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through an Akerez account system. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support@akerez.com, contact us and we will delete it from our email records when appropriate. Advertising and subscription availability may be limited by Apple’s age rating and platform rules.

12. Security

We rely on iOS sandboxing and standard Apple storage APIs for on-device data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock and keep iOS updated.

13. International availability

When ProVerbs is available on the App Store, it may be offered in multiple countries. On-device practice data is processed where your device is located. If you email support, your message is received by Diana Zaluzhna, operating as Akerez Software, and may be handled in the country where support email is accessed. When advertising is enabled, Google may process advertising-related information in the locations described in Google’s policies.

14. Your choices

  • Delete ProVerbs or its on-device data to remove local practice history
  • Change language and appearance in the app settings
  • When UMP ships in the app: use the in-app privacy options control when Google requires it
  • If ATT is shown in a future configuration: respond to Apple’s prompt
  • When the subscription is offered: subscribe, restore purchases, or manage / cancel through Apple
  • Contact us at support@akerez.com with privacy questions

15. Changes to this policy

We will revise this Privacy Policy when the monetised build’s code and AdMob configuration are verified, when this draft notice is removed for publication, and whenever ProVerbs or this website changes in a way that affects privacy. The date at the top of the page will be updated when changes are published.
