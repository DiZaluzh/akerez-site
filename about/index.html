About

An independent practice for software.

AKEREZ makes digital products. The work begins with an idea and continues through design, building, and the patience to keep going.

What comes first

Own products come first: software people can return to. Verbs and Invoice Book are the current products. The catalogue is meant to grow.

How the work grows

AKEREZ holds a quiet idea: something small, begun with intention, can become something people rely on. That stays in the work itself — how a product starts, takes shape, and stays in use — rather than in decoration.

Custom work

We sometimes design and build websites, web applications, mobile applications, and custom software. We support the software we create. We do not take on repair of other people’s projects, generic support, or maintenance of code we did not write.

The name

AKEREZ is never translated. It stays AKEREZ in English, French, and Ukrainian.
